# Sales Analytics Excel Dashboard
## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales data and presenting key business metrics through an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard.
The dashboard helps users explore sales performance across regions, product categories, sales channels, customer types, and time periods.
## Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Compare revenue across regions and product categories.
- Track monthly revenue trends.
- Understand sales distribution by customer type and sales channel.
- Identify the top products by revenue.
- Present business information through an interactive dashboard.
## Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel slicers and date timelines
- SQL for sales analysis

## Key Performance Indicators
The dashboard displays:
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
## Dashboard Features
- Region filter
- Sales channel filter
- Product category filter
- Customer type filter
- Date filters for year, quarter, month, and day
- Revenue trend by month
- Revenue comparison by region
- Revenue by customer type and sales channel
- Revenue by product category
- Top 5 products by revenue

-  Dashboard-Screenshots/ - Dashboard preview images

## Key Insights
Central region generates the highest revenue ($803K).
Computers are the highest-revenue product category ($2.06M).
Gaming Laptop is the top product by revenue ($666K).
Online sales generate the highest revenue among sales channels ($1.11M).
Returning customers account for 68% of the customer distribution.
West region generates the lowest revenue ($587K) among the five regions.

## How to Use
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the Excel workbook in Microsoft Excel.
3. Use the available filters and timelines to explore the dashboard.
4. Review the SQL folder for the analysis queries, if applicable.
## Limitations
The results depend on the quality and coverage of the available dataset.
The dashboard presents descriptive sales analysis and does not establish the causes of sales trends.
